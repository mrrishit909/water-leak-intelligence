Water Leak Intelligence

Forty districts of an invented water network. One is leaking, one has a bad sensor, one just had a busy night. Which is which, where is the leak, and which pipes should be replaced first? …
The utility, its pipes, failures and sensor readings are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real network. Hydraulics are linearised, not EPANET. Costs are stated assumptions. No outside model or service is called.
